# Review replacement after staff removal

## Reference and intended behavior

Furnished Finder's [Editing, Deleting, or Resubmitting a Review](https://support.furnishedfinder.com/hc/en-us/articles/35747032949787-Editing-Deleting-or-Resubmitting-a-Review), updated April 24, 2026 and read October 25, says published reviews cannot be directly edited/deleted by authors. Authors may request removal, and after removal submit a new review. Declined reviews may also be revised and resubmitted. We do not adopt a support response-time promise without an operating team.

Our existing app allowed rejected/legacy resubmission but permanently blocked removed authors. A removed review now offers **Submit replacement** in My reviews and the property/traveler review form. The author reviews the text, supplies valid stay context/contact details and explicitly reconfirms first-hand experience. It goes through fresh staff moderation. Published or pending text remains immutable to its author.

## Persistence and authority

The existing canonical review ID and unique author/property or author/traveler binding remain. Each author submission advances its revision; the client must provide the exact current revision for any existing record. Missing/stale revision values fail, including after later removal cycles. Concurrent attempts cannot create two current reviews or overwrite a pending submission. The latest submission timestamp is shown, while original creation and prior text/status/reply revisions remain in the staff audit snapshots. This is a new moderated submission under the existing history, not an unmoderated edit of published content.

Replacement clears the current response body, status, timestamp and decision note. The old response and approval stay in the private audit. A fresh response requires fresh submission/moderation and a later response revision; stale staff decisions cannot approve an old reply under new text. A removed review is already excluded from rating totals. Its replacement contributes nothing until approved; approval counts its new rating once. Staff removal notices explain the replacement path. Unavailable property/subject context withholds the action and requires support instead of opening a broken form.

Private contact phone, stay evidence, staff snapshots and internal revisions do not enter public projections. A pending replacement is not shown in received/public feeds. Staff authenticity investigation and content judgment remain necessary; an accepted inquiry or test success does not establish a real stay.

## Verification boundary

Source tests cover property and traveler replacements, current revision requirements, fresh attestation, unauthorized writes, pending/public separation, old-response snapshots/approval fences, rating totals, later removal/concurrent replacement cycles and unavailable context. The local native smoke uses guarded synthetic publication prerequisites, actual native functions and staff decisions; it retires its synthetic replacement and returns rating totals to baseline. Local synthetic identities are not Clerk browser authentication.

The hosted development fixture uses actual reserved Clerk sessions and an unpublished disposable rental. Imported private pending contact and removed review/reply history are explicit preconditions. They assert no real stay, public approval, paid/identity grant or customer experience. The owner browser may open/edit/cancel the form without accepting a first-hand attestation. A separately labeled native owner call can test persistence; temporary reserved staff only remove the synthetic replacement for cleanup. No synthetic review is approved/published in hosted development. Resend must be absent. Cleanup withdraws only the fixture discussion, removes its rental and disposable Clerk actors, revokes staff authority through signed offboarding and retains the removed review/audit under normal history rules.

Full real review submission/authenticity investigation, staff browser moderation, delivery to a real mailbox and whole-product acceptance are separate and remain open.
